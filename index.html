Midterm Review Sprint

Practice every skill on the midterm: vocabulary, prompts, error spotting, responsible use, and your B2–B4 option. Work in teams of three, then alone, then share.

Reader reads each item aloud · Checker asks "How do we know?" before you click Check · Reporter types the team answers and shares in Activity 6. Rotate roles if your teacher says so.

Activity 4 — Error Hunt

Team of 3 12 minutes · Part A, Section 3
12:00

Goal

Use a fact sheet to find AI errors and name their type.

Steps

  1. Compare each AI sentence with the fact sheet. Choose the type. Check.
  2. Correct two errors with evidence language.
  3. Rate the AI text's reliability as a team.

Done when

7/7 classified, 2 corrections, rating given.

Verified fact sheet — Riverside Language School

    Fictional school — practice only.

    Error types (Week 3)

    • Hallucination — invented facts, sources or people
    • Outdated — was true, not now
    • Unsupported claim — no evidence ("best", "everyone")
    • Bias — unfair to a group
    • Calculation error — wrong maths

    1AI output — accurate, or which error?

    2Correct two errors. Start with evidence language.

    Starters: "According to the fact sheet, …" · "The data show that …" · "I could not find evidence that …"

    3How reliable is this AI text? (1 = not reliable, 5 = very reliable)
